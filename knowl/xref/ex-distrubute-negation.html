Example 0.1.7.

Are \(\lnot (p \land q)\) and \(\lnot p \land \lnot q\) logically equivalent? That is, can you distribute the negation over the conjunction?
🔗
Solution.
Consider the truth table for \(\lnot (p \land q)\) and \(\lnot p \land \lnot q\text{:}\)
🔗
\(p\) \(q\) \(p \land q\) \(\lnot (p \land q)\) \(\lnot p\) \(\lnot q\) \(\lnot p \land \lnot q\)
\(T\) \(T\) \(T\) \(F\) \(F\) \(F\) \(F\)
\(T\) \(F\) \(F\) \(T\) \(F\) \(T\) \(F\)
\(F\) \(T\) \(F\) \(T\) \(T\) \(F\) \(F\)
\(F\) \(F\) \(F\) \(T\) \(T\) \(T\) \(T\)
The two columns are not identical:
🔗
\(p\) \(q\) \(\lnot (p \land q)\) \(\lnot p \land \lnot q\)
\(T\) \(T\) \(F\) \(F\)
\(T\) \(F\) \(T\) \(F\)
\(F\) \(T\) \(T\) \(F\)
\(F\) \(F\) \(T\) \(T\)
Hence \(\lnot (p \land q) \not\equiv \lnot p \land \lnot q\text{,}\) so you cannot distribute negations over conjunctions.
